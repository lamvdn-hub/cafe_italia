Simon has run this counter since 2008.

He cooks, pulls the espresso and knows the regulars by their order. Everything on the menu is made here, in a small kitchen behind the counter.

Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel ceiling greets you at the door. Out on the terrace, the view runs across the river to the boats and the Stena ferry heading out to sea.

From first espresso to last grappa.

  1. 10:00

    Caffè

    Espresso and cappuccino at the counter, with focaccia or a grilled panino. Weekends from 11:00.

  2. 11-14

    Pranzo

    Weekday lunch. Six classic pastas, four salads, and the lasagne the regulars come back for.

  3. 15:00

  4. Until 20:00
